What you can do

Every feature below comes from your C++ project.

Dashboard

See your profile, totals and recent workouts at a glance.

From C++: ProgressAnalysis report

BMI and calories

Check your BMI category and a daily calorie target for Bulk, Cut or Maintain.

From C++: calculateBMI() and dailyCalories()

Workout management

Add, search, update, delete, sort and undo your workouts.

From C++: FitnessTracker class with a linked list

Smart recommendations

Choose a goal, level and time to get a warm-up, main workout and cool-down.

From C++: recommendation_module.cpp

Weekly schedule

Pick 3 to 6 workout days and get a plan with rest days built in.

From C++: weekly_schedule_module.cpp

Analytics

Weekly graphs of minutes and calories across your workouts.

New on the web: each workout now gets a date
